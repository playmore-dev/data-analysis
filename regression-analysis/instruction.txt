## 2. Main Analytical Questions

The primary analytical question is:

> **Can residential property sale prices be predicted from the physical characteristics, quality, size, age, and location-related characteristics of a property?**

Investigate the following supporting questions:

1. Which property characteristics have the strongest relationship with sale price?
2. What characteristics appear to be associated with higher or lower property prices?
3. Are there important nonlinear relationships between property characteristics and price?
4. How well can regression models predict sale price for previously unseen properties?
5. Does a more complex nonlinear model substantially improve predictive performance compared with simpler linear models?
6. What are the major limitations and potential sources of prediction error?

Do not assume that correlation or feature importance establishes causation. Clearly distinguish **association, prediction, and causation**.

# 8. Model Evaluation

Evaluate all models using:

### Primary metrics

* **MAE — Mean Absolute Error**
* **RMSE — Root Mean Squared Error**
* **R² — Coefficient of Determination**

Explain what each metric means in the context of predicting house prices.

MAE should be interpreted as the typical magnitude of prediction error in the target variable's units.

RMSE should be used to show the impact of larger prediction errors.

R² should describe the proportion of variation in sale prices explained by the model, while explicitly noting that it does not directly represent prediction accuracy.

Where appropriate, compare both:

* Performance on training data
* Performance on unseen test data

Look for signs of overfitting.
